One device, large

It starts as its drawing and becomes the 3D view without a jump. Drag to turn it, or use the arrow keys; double-click or Home puts it back.

Build a computer from parts

Pick a board, a case and a hat. Every choice is checked for fit by real sizes and mount types, and one description makes the mesh and the drawing.

    To scale

    Each drawing fills its own picture by default. With scale="real" machines stand side by side at their true relative size on one baseline, and the lines keep the same width at any size.
